Research

I work on post-training large multimodal models: reinforcement learning for reasoning and perception, verifiable rewards, and the data and evaluation pipelines behind open model releases. A growing part of my work is on multimodal agents and generation.

About

I am currently a Postdoctoral Research Associate at Imperial College London, working with Prof. Jiankang Deng and Prof. Stefanos Zafeiriou. I received my Ph.D. from Zhejiang University, advised by Prof. Chao Wu and co-supervised by Prof. Kun Kuang and Prof. Fei Wu, and was fortunate to collaborate with Yibing Song at Alibaba DAMO Academy.
